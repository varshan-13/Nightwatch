[ 02 / HOW IT DECIDES ]

The model ranks the ocean. An officer makes the call.

01

Detect

Satellite radar spots every hull. Radar dots with no matching AIS signal become dark-vessel contacts.

02

Profile

Tracking gaps, meetings with carriers, loitering and port visits are added up into one profile per vessel.

03

Score

An anomaly model learns what normal looks like here and scores each vessel 0 to 100, with its top three reasons.

04

Decide

Officers confirm or dismiss each alert. Their feedback is logged and used to check the model.

[ 04 / LIMITS WE TAKE SERIOUSLY ]

A flag is a clue, not a verdict.

INNOCENT GAPS

Ships switch off AIS to avoid pirates, and signals drop out far from land. A gap alone never makes a high score.

NO FLAG PROFILING

A vessel's flag country is never used to raise its score. Behaviour is.
